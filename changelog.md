## v0.8.0 — 2026-11-23
- Implemented **Housing Behavior, Preferences, and Survival (`npc-realengine` Part 3)**:
- Seeded permanent, slowly evolving **NPC Housing Preference Profiles** (`preferredType`, `preferredDistrict`, `maxRentRatio`, `roommateTolerance`, annual drift on tier climbs, evictions, and 2+ year attachments) plus a weighted **5-factor Housing Satisfaction Score (0–100)** across Affordability (`40%`), Type Match (`20%`), District Match (`15%`), Condition (`10%`), and Roommates (`15%`).
- Added **Active Housing Evaluation & Move Decisions**: citizens re-evaluate every 30 days or immediately on homelessness, eviction, job/pay shifts (`>=20%`), tier changes, or steep rent hikes; factor in ∆70 same-district / ∆160 cross-district moving costs, patience vs impulsiveness thresholds, 2+ year loyalty stability, ambition-driven upgrades, and pride-resisted vs survival-forced downgrades with full Decision factor logs and Sharity reactions (`upgrade_flex`, `downgrade_shame`).
- Added **Roommate Compatibility, Monthly Friction & Friend Rescue**: `canJoinAsRoommate` checks affinity (`>30` applicant, `>20` / cost-split / kindness `>60` host) and roommate tolerance with desperate survival overrides; monthly neatness-gap (`>50`) and negative-affinity clashes erode affinity, spike anger, and trigger move searches; homeless citizens now ask up to 3 close friends (`affinity > 60`) for shelter with kindness-scaled rent discounts (`0%`/`50%`/`100%`), Decisions, gratitude pulses, and `friend_shelter_help` posts.
- Added **Dynamic Housing Market & Crisis Sell-Back**: financially distressed owners sell property back to the government at 85% of original purchase price with logged motivations and `sold-house` events/posts; monthly district vacancy shifts rents (`<10%` vacancy `+2–5%`, `>30%` vacancy `-2–4%`, `<50` condition drop), 6-month lease renewals apply landlord rate hikes (`>15%` triggers search & `rent-raise` posts), and buying prices track demand within ±10%/month.
- Connected **Housing Outcomes Across City Systems**: satisfaction `>75` boosts immunity/mental recovery, reduces sleep energy drain (`0.85x`), raises job performance (`+4`), and accelerates skill growth (`1.1x`); satisfaction `<30` lowers immunity/mental health, increases illness risk (`+20%`), hurts job performance (`-6`), freezes skill growth (`0x`), and drags decision quality; homelessness (`-15`) and eviction records (`-10`) penalize job candidate scoring.
- Expanded the **Housing** and **Citizen** UI with **Preferences (50)** (clickable filters opening Citizen Housing directly), **Market Dynamics** (5-district vacancy/rent trends + multi-type buying price chart), **Satisfaction Breakdown + "Would move if..."** panel inside Citizen Housing, **5 new SimGod Housing Powers** (force rent hike, district boom/bust, force sell-back, N-house/N-day shortage, force roommates), and `v0.7.0 -> v0.8.0` migration.
