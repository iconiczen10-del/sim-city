# P1: 3-Month City Extinction Root-Cause Analysis

**Status:** Diagnosed (Analysis Only — No Code Modified)  
**Symptom:** Across normal runs with no SimGod intervention, **Vij Mart goes insolvent at the end of Month 3 (`closedUntilFunded = true`) and 100% of all 50 citizens die within ~90–105 days (`M3D30`–`M4D15`)**, overwhelmingly from dehydration.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The ~3-month extinction is **not** caused by a single broken formula. It is a **connected, self-reinforcing economic death spiral** linking citizen shopping behavior, Mart demand forecasting, ordering logic, clearance pricing, rush fees, end-of-month cash priority, and dehydration lethality:

1. **Bullwhip Over-Ordering & Spoilage:** Wealthier/disciplined citizens batch-buy up to 15–21 meals at once while Mart's forecast treats that single-day spike as baseline daily demand (`dailyForecast` inflates to `70–190+/day` vs. true population consumption of `~120–150` meals across all tiers). Mart over-orders food that spoils after 4 days (`~1,560–2,000` meals/month thrown away).
2. **Below-Cost Clearance Death Spiral:** Between citizen batch-buying pauses, Mart sits on bloated 11+ day inventory and near-expiry food, triggering `clearance` mood (`-4%`/`-2%` margin) and lowering the price floor from `1.0 × buyCost` to **`0.75 × buyCost`**. Combined negative multipliers force Vij Mart to sell thousands of essential units at a **25% loss below wholesale cost**.
3. **Per-SKU `∆100` Rush Fee Drain:** Every stock-out SKU independently pays `MART_RUSH_FEE = ∆100` (not per trip), burning `~∆4,000–∆5,000` in pure overhead across the first 2–3 months.
4. **Cash-Reserve Bypass & Month-End Execution Order:** `processMartOrdering` bypasses the cash reserve floor for `food` and `water`, spending `mart.cash` down to `< ∆5`. Worse, on Day 30, `processMartDayEnd` runs ordering **before** `runCityMonthEnd` deducts `MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST = ∆1,500`, guaranteeing missed operating payments at Month 2 end (`unpaidOpMonths = 1`) and Month 3 end (`unpaidOpMonths = 2`).
5. **Permanent Closure & Rapid Mass Dehydration:** At `unpaidOpMonths >= 2` (Month 3 Day 30), `mart.closedUntilFunded = true` locks all citizens—including wealthy CEOs with `∆20,000+` savings—out of buying water and food. Because citizens lose `-45` thirst/day when dry and `-34` to `-40.8` health/day at `thirst === 0`, the entire surviving city dies within 5–10 days of closure (or earlier in Month 3 once `mart.cash` hits `~∆0` and water can no longer be ordered).

## 4. Detailed Root-Cause Breakdown

### Root Cause 1: Bullwhip Forecast Spike + Citizen Batch/Panic Buying -> Massive Food Spoilage
**Files:** `dailyCitizen.ts`, `martShopping.ts`, `martAI.ts`, `martOrdering.ts`, `martEngine.ts`

1. **Synchronized Citizen Batch & Panic Buying (`dailyCitizen.ts` lines 78–97, `martShopping.ts` line 54):**
   - Any citizen with `savings >= 600 || traits.discipline >= 60` (`isBufferBuyer`) buys up to `15` meals (`5` days) whenever `totalMeals < 3` (actually up to **3–4 days** of food even though food spoils in **4 days**).
   - If *any* preferred SKU hits `0` stock even once, `martShopping.ts` sets `citizen.stockoutMemoryDays = 60` (a full 2 months!).
   - While `stockoutMemoryDays > 0`, the citizen's trigger threshold doubles (`totalMeals < 6`), and they add `panicFood` (`+3` or `+6` meals), requesting up to **`18–21` meals in a single transaction**—which also exceeds the 4-day food shelf life, causing citizen-side spoilage too.
2. **Forecast Amplification (`martAI.ts` lines 17–31):**
   - `updateSalesForecasts` takes the 7-day mean (`avg`), multiplies by `trend = clamp(recentAvg / olderAvg, 0.75, 1.35)`, multiplies by `paydaySpike = 1.18` on days 28–3, and multiplies by `noise` (`0.9–1.1`).
   - Because buffer buyers buy `15–21` meals on one day and `0` on the next 4–5 days, `salesHistory` oscillates wildly (`[0, 0, 0, 0, 95, 110, 0]`). When a batch wave lands in `recent` (last 2 days), `recentAvg / olderAvg` hits the `1.35` cap while `paydaySpike` adds `1.18` (`1.35 × 1.18 = 1.593x`).
   - That pushes `f1.dailyForecast` to **`70–190` meals/day**, even when the entire city only has ~15–25 L1 eaters (`45–75` meals/day).
3. **Over-Ordering Into a Demand Vacuum (`martOrdering.ts` lines 47–61):**
   - Reorder point is `dailyForecast * (gap + 2) + safetyStock` (for food `gap = 2`, ~4.8+ days of inflated forecast).
   - `maxShelfCap = Math.ceil(item.dailyForecast * (FOOD_SPOIL_DAYS - 0.3))` allows ordering up to **`3.7 × dailyForecast`** in a single delivery.
   - Delivery arrives 2 days later (`MART_LEAD_TIME_DAYS = 2`)—right when the buffer-buying citizens are already full and stop buying food for 4 days.
4. **Spoilage Wipeout (`martEngine.ts` lines 71–95):**
   - Unsold Mart food expires after `FOOD_SPOIL_DAYS = 4` (`item.foodAges[3]` discarded). In Month 1 alone, **`1,560` meals** spoil (`5,174` meals over 3 months), burning thousands of `∆` in wholesale cash with zero revenue recovery.

### Root Cause 2: Clearance Mood + Sub-Cost Floor (`0.75 * buyCost`) Drives Guaranteed Negative Margins
**Files:** `martAI.ts`, `martPricing.ts`

1. **False Surplus Detection (`martAI.ts` lines 40–49, 87–91):**
   - Between buying waves (or when `foodAges[2] > 8`, which happens constantly after over-ordering), `pickCategoryMood` forces `clearance` (`marginPct: -4` on near-expiry food, or `marginPct: -2` when `avgDaysLeft > 11`).
2. **Sub-Cost Price Floor (`martPricing.ts` lines 21–46):**
   - When `mood === 'clearance'`, the price floor drops from `item.buyCost` to **`item.buyCost * 0.75`**.
   - At the same time, surplus stock (`stockAdjPct` down to `-22%`), near-expiry (`expiryAdjPct = -15%`), low 2-day sales vs. bloated `dailyForecast` (`demandAdjPct` down to `-12%`), and falling citizen savings/mood (`affordAdjPct` down to `-15%`) compound multiplicatively:
     - Example: `buyCost * 0.96 * 0.78 * 0.85 * 0.88 * 0.85 ≈ 0.48 * buyCost`, clamping at **`0.75 * buyCost`**.
   - As observed in the diagnostic run:
     - `f1` (`buyCost = ∆1.30`, base sell `∆2.00`) dropped to **`∆0.98–∆1.12`** (losing `∆0.18–∆0.32` per meal sold).
     - `w1` (`buyCost = ∆0.33`, base sell `∆0.50`) dropped to **`∆0.25–∆0.26`** (losing `∆0.07–∆0.08` per day sold).
   - Across 3 months, **`9,928` units** (nearly all high-volume essentials) were sold **below wholesale cost**, turning Vij Mart's highest-turnover SKUs into guaranteed cash drains.

### Root Cause 3: Per-SKU `∆100` Emergency Rush Fee Bleeds Residual Cash
**Files:** `martOrdering.ts`, `constants.ts`

- In `martOrdering.ts` lines 40–68:
  - Regular `MART_TRIP_FEE` (`∆40`) is charged once per category (`tripPaid`), **but `MART_RUSH_FEE` (`∆100`) is charged per SKU** whenever `item.quantity === 0`:
    ```ts
    const isEmergency = item.quantity === 0;
    const rushFee = isEmergency ? MART_RUSH_FEE : 0;
    ```
  - Worse, `rawNeed` is clamped up to at least `2` (L5) or `4` (L1–L4) units (`clamp(rawNeed, item.standard === 5 ? 2 : 4, maxShelfCap)`), so a stocked-out low-volume SKU like `Household L5` or `Food L4` can trigger a **`∆100` rush fee** on an order of just `2–4` units—multiple times a week.
  - Because `isEmergency` fires whenever `quantity === 0` regardless of `mart.cash` health or whether `dailyForecast` justifies a rush fee, Vij Mart pays **`~∆4,800` in rush fees** across 48 emergency orders in 3 months—**more than 3 months of total store operating rent (`3 × ∆1,500 = ∆4,500`)**.

---

### Root Cause 4: Essential-Order Reserve Bypass + Day-30 Execution Order Bug
**Files:** `martOrdering.ts`, `simulation.ts`, `monthlyCycle.ts`

1. **Essentials Bypass `reserveFloor` (`martOrdering.ts` lines 32, 70–71):**
   ```ts
   const reserveFloor = mart.cash > MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST * 1.4 ? MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST * 0.5 : 0;
   ...
   const isEssential = cat === 'food' || cat === 'water';
   const spendLimit = isEssential ? mart.cash : Math.max(0, mart.cash - reserveFloor);
   ```
   - `reserveFloor` is only `∆750` (half of `MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST = ∆1,500`) and drops to `∆0` whenever `mart.cash <= ∆2,100`.
   - Worse, `food` and `water` ignore `reserveFloor` completely (`spendLimit = mart.cash`), allowing Mart to spend its cash down to **`∆0.26–∆2.13`** on a single day.
2. **Order of Operations on Day 30 (`simulation.ts` lines 86–97, `monthlyCycle.ts` lines 96–109):**
   - On `day === DAYS_PER_MONTH` (`day === 30`), `simulation.ts` executes:
     1. `processMartDayEnd(...)` -> calls `processMartOrdering(...)` -> spends remaining cash on wholesale orders down to `~∆0–∆2`.
     2. `runCityMonthEnd(...)` -> checks `if (mart.cash >= MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST)` (`>= ∆1,500`).
   - Because `processMartOrdering` just spent any cash remaining at the end of Day 30 before `runCityMonthEnd` checked operating costs, Mart fails the `∆1,500` op-cost check at **Month 2 End** (`unpaidOpMonths = 1`) and **Month 3 End** (`unpaidOpMonths = 2`), triggering **`mart.closedUntilFunded = true`**.
   - Even if Mart had `∆1,500+` revenue on Day 30, `processMartOrdering` can spend it on new stock minutes (in execution order) before operating rent is collected.

### Root Cause 5: Permanent Insolvency Closure -> Total City Dehydration
**Files:** `monthlyCycle.ts`, `martShopping.ts`, `dailyCitizen.ts`, `citizenConsumption.ts`

1. **Irrecoverable Lockout (`monthlyCycle.ts` lines 101–108, `martShopping.ts` line 48):**
   - Once `unpaidOpMonths >= 2`, `mart.closedUntilFunded = true` permanently halts all sales (`if (mart.closedDaysLeft > 0 || mart.closedUntilFunded) return false;`), and `processMartOrdering` also returns immediately (`if (mart.closedDaysLeft > 0 || mart.closedUntilFunded) return;`).
   - There is only **one** shop in Vij. Even citizens with `∆20,000+` savings (T1 CEOs and T2 Executives) have zero source of water or food.
2. **Fast Dehydration Clock (`citizenConsumption.ts` line 66, `dailyCitizen.ts` lines 19–22):**
   - Every day, thirst drops `-45` (`citizen.thirst = clamp(citizen.thirst - 45, 0, 100)`). Once water inventory runs out (max `~7` days, often fewer once Mart cash drops to `∆0` in Month 3), a citizen hits `thirst === 0` within **2 days**.
   - At `thirst === 0`, health drops **`-34/day`** (or **`-40.8/day`** if `age > 55`).
   - Every citizen dies within **3 days of hitting `thirst === 0`**, wiping out 100% of the population between **Day 90 and Day 105** (`48` dehydration, `2` starvation in the verified trace).

### Root Cause 6: Early Month-1 Attrition & Idle Clothes Working-Capital Lockup (Contributing Multipliers)
**Files:** `seedProfiles.ts`, `dailyCitizen.ts`, `standards.ts`, `martSetup.ts`, `citizenConsumption.ts`

1. **Early Unemployed & Poor Citizen Deaths (`seedProfiles.ts` lines 99–103):**
   - 8 `Unemployed` citizens start with `∆20–∆145` savings and `∆0` monthly income, plus 1 `Entry` T5 citizen with `∆25–∆75`.
   - With monthly fixed costs of `∆180` (`shared_room` `∆80` + `FIXED_NON_RENT_COSTS` `∆100`), plus daily food/water purchases (~`∆195/month` at L1), these citizens hit `∆0` within 10–30 days and die of starvation/dehydration/illness in Month 1 (`5–9` dead by `M1D30`), shrinking Mart's paying customer base right when its cash crisis begins.
   - Additionally, every death inflicts a city-wide `grief` mood hit (`simulation.ts` lines 52–58), depressing `avgCitizenMood`, which in turn lowers `affordAdjPct` in `martPricing.ts`, pushing Mart prices even further downward into losses!
2. **Slow Clothes Turnover Locks Initial Capital (`martSetup.ts`, `citizenConsumption.ts` line 112, `dailyCitizen.ts` line 98):**
   - Citizens start with 2–4 outfits wearing down at `0.833%/day` (`120` days per outfit) and only buy clothes when `outfits.length === 0` or the last outfit drops `< 20%` condition.
   - Across the first 90+ days, virtually **no clothes sell**, yet `martSetup.ts` seeds 7 days of clothes across all 5 standards (including expensive L4/L5 outfits) and `estimateSeedDemand` keeps `dailyForecast >= 0.25`, tying up working capital and `∆40` trip fees on dead-stock restocks.

## 5. Design Proposal for Fixes (For Review Before `"go code"`)

When ready to patch in the next revision, fixes should address all legs of the loop simultaneously (fixing only one leg will still leave Mart unprofitable or unstable):

1. **Smooth Citizen Demand & Cap Spoilable Food Purchases (`dailyCitizen.ts`, `martShopping.ts`):**
   - Cap citizen `food` buffer target by remaining shelf life (e.g., max `6–9` meals = `2–3` days instead of `15–21` meals that spoil in citizen pantries), and reduce `stockoutMemoryDays` from `60` to `~10–14` days with moderate panic padding (`+1` to `+3` meals).
2. **Stabilize Mart Forecast (`martAI.ts`):**
   - Use an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) or median-filtered rolling window instead of letting a single 2-day batch-buying spike multiply `1.35 × 1.18 = 1.59x` across all future food orders.
3. **Eliminate Unprofitable Clearance & Sub-Cost Spiral (`martAI.ts`, `martPricing.ts`):**
   - Keep clearance floor at `buyCost * 0.92`–`0.95` (or only allow deep clearance `< 1.0 * buyCost` strictly on `food` items that are genuinely 1 day from spoiling, never on `water` which never spoils!).
   - Raise `avgDaysLeft > 11` clearance check so normal 4–7 day supply cushions don't trigger clearance, and don't let `mood === 'clearance'` force `w1` water to sell at `∆0.25` vs `∆0.33` cost.
4. **Fix Rush Fee Per-Trip & Guard Low-Cash Ordering (`martOrdering.ts`):**
   - Charge `MART_RUSH_FEE` at most once per category/delivery trip (like `tripPaid`), and disable discretionary rush fees when `mart.cash < MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST * 1.5` or on low-priority L3–L5 SKUs when L1/L2 stock exists.
5. **Enforce Operating Cost Priority & Day-30 Order of Operations (`simulation.ts`, `monthlyCycle.ts`, `martOrdering.ts`):**
   - Reserve `MART_MONTHLY_OP_COST` (`∆1,500`, scaled proportionally as Day 1 -> Day 30 progresses, or protected when `day >= 25`) from discretionary non-emergency spending, and run `runCityMonthEnd` operating expense settlement **or** reserve check properly relative to `processMartDayEnd`.
   - Consider emergency partial recovery / city bailout or auto-reopen loan/credit mechanic from Treasury (or SimGod prompt + grace handling) if cash recovers, plus ensuring `water` L1 has emergency protection so a cash crunch doesn't instantly dehydrate all 50 citizens.
6. **Review Unemployed Month-1 Survival (`seedProfiles.ts` / `economy.ts` / `monthlyCycle.ts`):**
   - Decide whether Unemployed citizens should receive minimal municipal welfare/relief from the city treasury (e.g., ∆250–∆350/month subsistence from `treasury`) or slightly higher starting buffer/lower L1 basic costs so Month 1 doesn't automatically kill 10–18% of the city and trigger grief/price deflation.
